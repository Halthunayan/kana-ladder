Sound check

Car mode plays everything out loud and asks nothing of your hands or your eyes. Connect the phone to the car first, then play the test.

Check the phone is paired and the car is on the Bluetooth source, then raise the volume while the test is actually playing, not before. iPhone stops all speech the moment this app leaves the screen, so keep it open and mounted: car mode holds the screen awake on its own.

Set it up before you drive, not while you are moving. Once it is running it needs neither your hands nor your eyes, and nothing in car mode touches your schedule: it is exposure, not a test.

How it runs: one side of a word plays, then silence. Say the other side out loud in that gap. The answer follows, so you hear straight away whether you had it. Nothing to press, nothing to read.
